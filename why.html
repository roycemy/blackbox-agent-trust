The reason / 001

Trust is not a setting.

Agent companies won't audit themselves. That's why the layer on your side matters.

We asked AI to write. Now we're asking it to act.

An answer can be revised. An email sent to the wrong person can't be unsent. A purchase can cost more than the price on screen.

Blind trust is simple until the first thing goes wrong. The answer isn't a hundred permission screens. It's an understandable record and a few boundaries that actually hold.

Receipts for what happened. Rules for what may happen. The Gate for what only you can decide.

Your assistant should work for you. The oversight should, too.
